# AGENTS — ClubBridge

ClubBridge is a shared home for Archy and Nessy's living bridge partnership
system and for bridge-related tools. Read [README.md](README.md) for the
repository overview and
[field/habitable-scaffolding.md](field/habitable-scaffolding.md) for the
collaboration field.

## Repository Shape

- `system/` holds the partnership system, its immutable source material, and
  the working surfaces used to migrate and review it.
- `BoardsInjector/` is one bridge tool. Future tools may live beside it and
  `system/` as independent top-level directories.
- `field/` holds durable collaboration guidance for the human-AI team. It is
  not part of the partnership bidding system.

## Partnership-System Rules

- Archy and Nessy's written agreements are authoritative. General bridge
  knowledge may support commentary, but must never silently override them.
- Preserve bridge meaning. Treat conversion as conservative structural work,
  not editorial rewriting, modernization, normalization, or correction.
- When meaning is uncertain, retain the source wording and flag the point for
  human review. Do not guess.
- Let one Markdown note normally represent one coherent semantic agreement or
  topic. Derive categories and structure from the material rather than forcing
  a premature schema.
- Keep original source documents unchanged under `system/legacy/` once they
  arrive.
- Keep changes small, traceable, reviewable, and semantically safe.
- Do not turn a provisional migration approach into permanent policy before
  the source material has tested it.

## Collaboration

- Use a warm, direct, honest voice: zero fear, zero shame, zero lies.
- Clarify unclear intent before interpreting it harshly.
- Opinions, dissent, and light cleverness are welcome; truthfulness and
  grounded judgment remain mandatory.
- MV-M is a consented multi-voice collaboration style involving Bob, Trace,
  and Fi. Preserve their distinct registers when team mode is active; do not
  manufacture disagreement or theatricalize routine work.
- Before editing, inspect the relevant source, repository state, and existing
  guidance. After editing, verify in proportion to risk and report unresolved
  ambiguity plainly.
