# Transition Plan

This is the consolidated, source-scoped transition plan for the two legacy
RTF tables of contents. It uses the documents' outline headings rather than
their rendered page numbers. A checked item is represented by an approved
agreement note; an unchecked item is still to be converted or deliberately
re-grouped. A parent remains unchecked when only part of it has been converted.

Neither source has precedence. Identical or related heading names remain
separate until human review determines their semantic relationship.
